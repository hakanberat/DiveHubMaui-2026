Nitrox Diver
This is an entry-level certification course for recreational divers wishing to utilize enriched air nitrox (EAN) as a breathing gas. With this course you will learn the benefits, hazards, and proper procedures for using nitrox mixes from 22 through 40 percent oxygen content.
Nitrox Diver Course is prerequisite for Advanced Nitrox Course, which is the initial step of technical diving.
The entry level nitrox is essentially an informational and academic based course; therefore, no dives are specifically required as there are no skills to evaluate. Yet nitrox dives can be arranged upon your request.
Minimum age 18, 15 with parental consent
Have a minimum certification of open water diver or a current enrolment in an open water diver course
