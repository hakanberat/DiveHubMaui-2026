CMAS 1*
This is the first course available under CMAS to achieve certification as an open water diver. Knowledge of basic diving theory is covered by video and instructor presentations, diving skills are developed in confined water and open water under the supervision of your instructor. By the end of the course you will have knowledge about the SCUBA equipment and dive theory; you will be ready for open water dives.
As CMAS 1* Diver you will have theoretical knowledge about: basic gear, basic diving signs, pressure-volume relations, SCUBA implementing, diving physics, diving physiology, first aid, safe diving applications, underwater life and diving tables. You will also gain skills to apply what you have learned in the classroom in theory.
Requirements:
minimum age is 14 (under age of 18 parent’s approval required)
to know how to swim
To be healthy and to sing the notice that there are no health
obstacles for Scuba diving (under age of 18 parents approval required)
hold at least primary degree in school
