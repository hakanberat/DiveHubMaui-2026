Night Dives
Why not try a night dive in the Eastern Mediterranean and experience a different kind of nightlife! Spanish dancers, delicate feather stars and octopi are just some of the nocturnally active critters you can spot after the sun goes down over Bogaz.
Diving at night reveals a whole new world on even a familiar reef or wreck. You’ll notice many underwater residents exhibit unique behaviours at twilight, bedding down for the night or positioning themselves for successful night time feeding. Some underwater inhabitants hide during the day and can only be seen at night. But, be sure to shine your light out into the bigger water around you occasionally to catch a passing stingray or a curious grouper.
Kit up on the boat after watching the sun set, and dive into a different world. Should you need a torch we will provide you with one for no extra charge.
We normally schedule a night dive at least once per week. We require a minimum of two people to offer a night dive. We recommend you sign up for a night dive at the dive centre on your first day, or ask your dive guide, so we can inform you of the next available trip.
