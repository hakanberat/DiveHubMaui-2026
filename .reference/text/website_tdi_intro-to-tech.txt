Intro to Tech
If you are not a technical diver and are wishing to access the deeper depths of the sea, or stay at regular depths longer with no decompression stops or shorten your decompression stops you will make good use of this course on your path of becoming a technical diver.
With this course, you will earn the preliminary knowledge necessary for technical divers such as technical equipment configurations, to enhance open water diving skills (such as buoyancy, trim, and situational awareness), and to introduce students to advanced gas planning techniques within a no-decompression context.
Includes 3 dives must be conducted; All dives must be conducted at depths within the diver’s current level of certification but no dives should exceed 23 metres
Prerequisites:
Minimum age 18, 15 with parental consent
Minimum certification an Open Water Scuba Diver or equivalent
Provide proof of 25 logged open water dives
Intro to Tech may be combined with TDI Advanced Nitrox.
3 dives, € 250
