Try Dives
You would like to visit the underwater world and discover the colourful underwater beauties for the first time? You don’t have to enrol on a course to sample the beauty of the Eastern Mediterranean Sea. Let one of our instructors take you on a guided underwater tour. We are blessed with crystal clear warm waters with little or no currents – which means we have excellent diving conditions.
We are offering you shore dives depths ranging between 3 and 5 metres, where you can swim with different species of fishes, caretta carettas, see colourful corals growing along the rocks, see moray eels and octopuses in their coves, get across a sea horse, see the curious squirrelfish and priest fish and schools, crabs of different colours running around and colourful parrotfish.
Boat Dives
We are always discovering new sites which gives you more of a variety of dive sites.
Night Dives
We normally schedule a night dive at least once per week. We require a minimum of two people to offer a night dive.
